A few simple ground rules.

Guidance on using our free inspiration and craft ideas.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Free editorial content

The Velvet December provides free, informational gift and decoration ideas. Nothing on this website is sold, and access to the guides does not require payment.

Use your judgment

Crafting, baking and decorating involve everyday risks. Follow product safety labels, supervise children, handle hot tools and ovens carefully, and keep decorations away from open flames. Check food allergies and storage guidance when making edible gifts.

Project estimates

Time, difficulty and cost labels are editorial estimates, not guarantees. Availability and prices of supplies vary.

Content

You may use these ideas for personal projects and share links to the guides. Please do not republish the full text or claim it as your own.

Changes

We may revise or remove content as necessary. Review these terms periodically.

Questions

Contact us using the details on our contact page.
