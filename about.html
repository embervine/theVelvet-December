Welcome to The Velvet December

The Velvet December is a small, independent seasonal idea journal focused on meaningful gift giving, approachable home decorating and the joy of making something yourself.

Our aim is to help you feel inspired rather than overwhelmed. You do not need a big budget, specialist tools or a picture-perfect home to create a lovely holiday atmosphere.

What you’ll find here

Our free guides offer practical material lists, clear step-by-step instructions, estimated costs and tips for making projects your own. Cost estimates are approximate and vary by location and supplies.

Our approach

We favor resourceful ideas, thoughtful gestures and accessible projects. This is an independent editorial website, not a storefront, and you do not need to pay to read any article.
